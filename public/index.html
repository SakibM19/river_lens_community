Document a visible observation

Upload one photo of a river, canal or pond. RiverLens describes only what is visible. It does not test the water, judge safety or name a source of pollution.

Choose a photo or drop it here
JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 12 MB. Location data is removed automatically.

Privacy: the photo is resized and stripped of location data in your browser. It is then saved in this site's shared database and, when AI analysis is on, sent to the AI provider to be described. Saved posts are visible to everyone who opens this site, so please do not include people's faces or private places.
